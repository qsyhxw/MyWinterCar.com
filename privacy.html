How analytics, advertising and contact information are handled on mywintercar.com

Updated October 29, 2026. This policy covers mywintercar.com, an independent fan guide site.

Cloudflare provides site delivery and may collect security, performance and request information, including its Web Analytics service. Your browser may also contact Google Fonts to load typefaces.

Advertising

Content pages include Google AdSense (publisher ca-pub-8830315294299920). Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure advertising, including personalized advertising where enabled and permitted. About, Contact, Privacy and error pages do not include the site's advertising loader. This policy does not claim that all advertising requests or consent settings have been independently audited.

Contact and external links

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to address your message. Do not send passwords or sensitive game-account details. External services have their own privacy policies. We do not operate a site account registration system.
